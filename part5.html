15. The Cost of Emotional Illiteracy

Emotional illiteracy fuels conflict, misunderstanding, and inner turmoil. The costs are personal, social, and societal.

16. Schooling the Emotions

Emotions can be taught. Emotional literacy programs in schools equip children with skills for life.

Aristotle's Challenge

“Anyone can become angry — that is easy. But to be angry with the right person, to the right degree, at the right time, for the right purpose, and in the right way — this is not easy.”

This is the essence of emotional mastery: applying wisdom to feeling.
