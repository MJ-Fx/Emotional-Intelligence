Part Two · Nature of EI

3. When Smart Is Dumb

Intelligence without emotional awareness can lead to poor decisions. High IQ is no guarantee of success; emotional intelligence often matters more.

4. Know Thyself

Self-awareness is the cornerstone of emotional intelligence. Recognizing our own emotions as they happen gives us choice.

5. Passion's Slaves

When emotions rule, we lose agency. Learning to manage passion without suppressing it is the art of emotional balance.

6. The Master Aptitude

Self-motivation, impulse control, and delay of gratification form the master aptitude that underpins achievement and well-being.

7. The Roots of Empathy

Empathy is the ability to read others' emotions. It is the foundation of social competence and compassion.

8. The Social Arts

Social intelligence is a set of skills — from influencing others to handling conflict — that shape our interactions.
